## Style Guidelines (for consistency in future assets)

- **Palette:** Near-black backgrounds, amber/warm gold accents, cold blue-green for the machine's glow
- **Mood:** Painterly cinematic. Not photorealistic. Not cartoonish. 
- **Time:** Contemporary (2023) but haunted by Victorian objects
- **People:** Shown from behind, partially, or not at all — Rowan is never shown face-on
- **No text in images** — all text is handled by Ren'Py
