# BEAT 1: THE PROLOGUE & ARRIVAL

*This script covers the opening cinematic narration and Rowan's physical arrival at the coastal rental. Her psychological baseline — Enneagram 5, Functional Isolation, the Materialist Vow — is established entirely through behavior and observation, never stated.*

---

## SCENE 1: THE ARCHIVE

**[SCENE START]**

*Visuals: Black screen. Then, slowly, paper — close enough to fill the frame. Victorian handwriting. Iron-gall ink blooming outward from the letters in purple halos where moisture has gotten into the fibers. The audio is nothing. Not silence. Pressure.*

**ROWAN (V.O.)**
> Most archives smell of acid and old glue.
> This one smelled of ozone.
> I should have noted that in my condition report. I didn't.

*Visuals: Fade in on Richmond House, 1856. Not a tableau — a working room. Amara Finch is crouched over a copper coil assembly, one ear tilted toward the glass cylinder, lips parted like she is deciding whether to hold a note. Thomas Rooke tightens a junction beneath the main coil, pencil tucked behind his ear, not watching Bell. Selina Deveraux writes in a ledger with one hand flat on the table, reading the vibration through the wood.*

*A candle burns on the table near Amara. She breathes on it — gently, experimentally. The flame bends toward the coils. She watches it. She does not write this down.*

**ROWAN (V.O.)**
> Richmond House, August 1856.
> The newspapers called it a seance. It wasn't.
> They were looking for a resonant frequency—a singular, Unitarian power flow to bind light and breath.

*Visuals: Professor Bell enters from the left. He does not go to the equipment. He watches it.*

**ROWAN (V.O.)**
> Their diaries say they found it.

*Visuals: The light in the room gathers — not toward the candle, but inward, toward the coils. A low vibration building. Then a bright flash, spreading outward from the equipment. Bell collapses. The others step back. Cut to black.*

**ROWAN (V.O.)**
> Not in the way they intended.

---

*Visuals: A study — books doubled on shelves, papers in stacks, two coffee mugs. Harrow is at the desk, working. He is hunched slightly forward, one hand holding a page flat, the other making notes in the margin. He does not look up. The manuscript box beside him is labeled: THE AETHERIC ARCHIVE — C. HARROW, ED. The camera moves slowly across his annotations: early notes in neat pencil, precise, academic. Then further along: the pencil presses harder. Historical citations are crossed out. In their place, columns of figures. Material tolerances. Wire gauges. Costs per foot.*

**ROWAN (V.O.)**
> Dr. Charles Harrow spent two years editing the archive.
> His footnotes became obsessive. He tracked one number across every page: 1985.
> Then he stopped writing footnotes.
> He started writing shopping lists.

*Visuals: A page of the manuscript. A circled gap. A margin note in red: *SOURCE?**

**ROWAN (V.O.)**
> My job was simple: verify the references, fill the gaps.
> One source wasn't there.
> Harrow cited a memorandum by Professor Bell — always at the critical junctures, never anywhere else.
> A document that might explain what happened that day.
> It wasn't in any of the files.

*Visuals: A phone screen. A series of unanswered emails. A brief text from someone labeled "DEPT": Rowan — just flag it and move on. Pub date is firm.*

**ROWAN (V.O.)**
> Months ago, Harrow stopped answering emails.
> The department told me to flag the gap and send it to print.
> But I tracked him down — a rental on the coast.
> I want answers. He has them.

**[SCENE END]**

## SCENE 2: THE ARRIVAL

**[SCENE START]**

*A taxi pulls up to the gate and stops. Rowan gets out. The cab pulls away.*

*In the narrow gravel drive beside the house: a small compact, dark blue, sat-nav sticker on the inside of the windscreen. Salt-misted. A city car.*

*Rowan stands at the gate. She is 32. She is in a nice coat over a thick-knit sweater, leather satchel over one shoulder, the galleys tucked under her arm. She clicks a brass mechanical pen. Click. Click. Click. She is reading the house the way she reads manuscripts: looking for what's been crossed out.*

**ROWAN (Internal Monologue)**
> *He didn't know I was coming.*

*She walks up the path. The satchel strap cuts into her shoulder. She doesn't adjust it.*

*Visuals: The front door. Pale blue paint, flaking. A brass knocker in the shape of an anchor. Beside it: a small panel of valves and a pressure dial — industrial hardware, wrong for a rental cottage.*

*Rowan looks at it. She turns the outermost valve. A low hiss — air equalizing.*

*The door releases.*

**ROWAN**
Dr. Harrow. It's Rowan Ellis.

*She steps inside.*

*Visuals: A thermometer mounted on the wall beside the coat rack. The red mercury column is dropping.*

*Audio: A sharp [SFX: ELECTRICAL POP]. The hallway light goes out.*

**[BEAT 1 END]**
