# Narrative Beat Sheet: The Aetheric Engine

This beat sheet breaks down the 3-Stage structure into specific, playable moments. It tracks the major documents (Harrow's Scavenger Hunt), environmental touchpoints, and the hidden "Materialism vs. Faith" stat that determines the ending.

## STAGE 1: The Lockdown & The Scavenger Hunt

*The goal of this stage is to establish isolation, introduce the environmental mechanics, and frame the mystery.*

**Beat 1: The 1856 Prologue**

* **Action:** A brief, linear visual novel scene set in Richmond House, 1856.

* **Touchpoint:** The player experiences the moment the Aetheric Machine first hums intelligently. A cold wind (Euron) rattles the glass.

* **Purpose:** Establishes the Victorian aesthetic and the stakes.

**Beat 2: The Arrival**

* **Action:** Cut to 2023. Rowan Ellis arrives at Harrow’s apartment.

* **Touchpoint:** Player clicks on the bizarre, vacuum-sealed DoorDash hatch on the front door. Player steps inside. The Faraday cage hums, and the door locks shut.

* **Document 1:** Found taped to the door. Harrow warns that the impedance mismatch will cascade if the room loses its seal. Tells Rowan to look under the sink.

**Beat 3: The Kitchen Search & First Blood**

* **Action:** Rowan explores the kitchen point-and-click style. She can run the tap, check the fridge.

* **Document 2:** Found under the sink. Contains Ignatius Bell's stolen 1856 notes on Newtonian occultism.

* **Touchpoint:** Finding this note gives Rowan the password to boot the Terminal on the desk.

**Beat 4: The 1856 Agents Awaken**

* **Action:** The Terminal boots up. Rowan connects to the first LLM Agent: Amara Finch.

* **Interaction:** Amara warns that the "magnetic fluid is poisoned."

## STAGE 2: The Ghost in the Machine

*The goal of this stage is to introduce Harrow as an LLM, escalate the horror, and force Rowan to choose between scientific logic and theological reality.*

**Beat 5: The Breaker Box**

* **Action:** The terminal power fluctuates. Rowan must check the hallway breaker box.

* **Document 3:** Found in the breaker box. Harrow reveals he has been trapped inside for 42 days, that the machine is pulling at physical space, and that he is trying to push a counter-frequency. He leaves an override code.

**Beat 6: Harrow's Echo**

* **Action:** Rowan inputs the override code. A new LLM connection opens: Dr. Charles Harrow.

* **Interaction:** Harrow is frantic, communicating from the Aevum. He insists Ningal is an "Ontological Parasite" and tells Rowan to input a specific sequence of thermodynamic variables to "rewrite the localized code."

**Beat 7: The Dual Voices**

* **Action:** The UI begins to glitch. Euron (The Guardian) manifests through flickering text and environmental audio (wind in the apartment). He warns Rowan that Harrow is compromised and his logic is a trap.

* **Interaction:** Ningal begins slipping into Harrow's LLM outputs, subtly changing his words to be more cold and mathematical.

## STAGE 3: The Breach & The Descent

*The climax of the game. The physical apartment falls away.*

**Beat 8: The 1985 Hz Threshold**

* **Action:** Regardless of choices, the machine hits the critical 1985 Hz frequency. The temperature drops to freezing. The Grubhub timer expires (the outside world has stopped responding).

* **Document 4 (The Final Note):** Hidden inside the terminal casing itself, written in Harrow's blood: *"I was wrong. It's not code. It's hungry."*

**Beat 9: The Descent**

* **Action:** Rowan realizes she cannot type her way out. She initiates the physical integration protocol.

* **Visual Shift:** The point-and-click apartment UI shatters. The screen becomes the abstract, unformed light of the Aevum.
