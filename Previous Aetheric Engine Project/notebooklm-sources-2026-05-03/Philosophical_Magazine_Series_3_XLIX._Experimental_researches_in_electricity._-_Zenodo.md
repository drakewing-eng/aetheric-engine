An excess of temperature of such amount and such continuance, must surely, one would suppose, have some sufficiently notable cause. I am not aware that any probable cause has yet been suggested; but should you oblige me by inserting this com- munication, it may at least be of use in commencing the dis- cussion, and possibly in eliciting the opinions of others, whose views on the subject the public may naturally desire to know.
