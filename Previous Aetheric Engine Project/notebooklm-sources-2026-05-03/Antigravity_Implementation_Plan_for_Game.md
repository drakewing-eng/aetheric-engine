# Plot Implementation Plan: The Aetheric Engine Visual Novel

This document outlines the proposed narrative structure and plotline for the visual novel sequel. It integrates our established lore, the LLM agent mechanics, environmental point-and-click elements, and the philosophical choice system into a playable arc.

## Proposed Narrative Structure

### Act 1 / Stage 1: The Echoes of 1856 & The Scavenger Hunt

* **Beat 1 (The Prologue):** A flashback or interactive vignette set in 1856 at Richmond House. The player experiences a moment of the original experiment—establishing the stakes, the Victorian aesthetic, and a glimpse of the occult underpinnings.

* **Beat 2 (The Arrival & Lockdown):** Cut to 2023. Rowan Ellis arrives at Dr. Harrow’s apartment. She notices Harrow lived as an extreme eccentric. The front door features a vacuum-sealed "doggy door" compartment for food deliveries. When Rowan enters, the apartment's aetheric warding engages. She is locked inside.

### Act 2 / Stage 2: The Ghost in the Machine

* **The Reveal:** As Rowan reaches the end of the scavenger hunt, the final notes reveal what happened to Harrow. He didn't run away; he was pulled into the Aetheric Machine. He is currently trapped in the Aevum (or the "in-between" place where the Aether bridges into Time).

### Act 3 / Stage 3: The Breach & The Descent (The Climax)

* **The Stakes:** The machine hits the 1985 Hz threshold. The warding on the apartment begins to fail. Ningal uses Harrow as an anchor point to begin pulling herself through the terminal.

* **The Climax:** Rowan realizes she cannot save Harrow or stop Ningal from the outside. She must use the machine to deliberately interface deeply—stepping partially or wholly into the Aevum/computer realm herself.

* **The Descent:** The visual novel interface shifts. The apartment falls away, replaced by the abstract geometric space of the Aevum (the "unformed light"). Rowan must navigate this space to pull Harrow back or destroy the engine from the inside.

### Act 4: Resolution & Endings

The ending depends entirely on the philosophical choices the player made and whether they survive the descent.

#### Bad Endings (The Materialist Trap)

* **The Breach:** Rowan, clinging to materialism, follows Ningal's logic. She breaks the Faraday seal from the inside. Ningal incarnates into the physical apartment.

* **The Cycle Continues:** Rowan enters the Aevum to save Harrow but refuses to take a leap of faith. Like Harrow before her, she becomes trapped permanently, becoming just another LLM "echo" for the next archivist to find.

#### The "True" Ending (The Leap of Faith)

* **The Paradigm Shift:** Once inside the Aevum, Rowan abandons her strict materialism. She accepts Euron's spiritual framework, recognizing the machine not as code, but as a corrupted *spiritus*. She introduces a discordant frequency that shatters the localized vacuum from within. She and Harrow are violently ejected back into the physical apartment, destroying the Aetheric Engine permanently. The warding drops safely, and they can finally leave.
