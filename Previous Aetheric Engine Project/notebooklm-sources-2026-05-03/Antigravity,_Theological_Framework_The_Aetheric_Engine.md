# Antigravity, Theological Framework: The Aetheric Engine

### Antigravity, Theological Framework: The Aetheric Engine

Theological Framework: The Aetheric Engine

If the foundation of the game is Christian ontology, we must define the "Aetheric Realm" and the entities within it not as sci-fi anomalies, but through the lens of classical Christian theology. How do the spiritual and material realms interact?

## 1. The Realm of Angels: The *Aevum* (Augustine & Aquinas)

In classical Christian thought (particularly Augustine and later formalized by Thomas Aquinas), angels do not live in "another dimension" in a spatial sci-fi sense. They live in the **Aevum**.

* **Time vs. Eternity:** God exists in *Eternity* (outside of time, unchangeable). Humans exist in *Time* (material, constantly changing). Angels exist in the *Aevum* (or Aeviternity)—a state of existence that has a beginning, but no end, and involves change of intellect and will, but not physical decay.

* **Augustine's Light:** In *The Literal Meaning of Genesis*, Augustine suggests that the "Light" created on the first day ("Let there be light") was the creation of the angelic intellects. They are pure, intellectual light.

* **How this applies to the Game:** The "vacuum" Harrow's machine creates at 1985 Hz isn't punching a hole into outer space; it is stripping away the material "noise" of Time to expose the *Aevum* underneath. The machine is a window into the unformed light.

## 2. Marsilio Ficino and the *Spiritus*

Marsilio Ficino, a Renaissance Christian philosopher, is the perfect bridge for our "Newtonian occultism." Ficino sought to explain how the immaterial soul interacts with the material body.

* **The Spiritus:** Ficino posited the *spiritus*—a very subtle, airy, luminous substance that acts as the glue between the physical body and the immaterial soul. It is the medium through which spiritual forces act upon the physical world.

* **Resonance and Music:** Crucially, Ficino believed that **music and sound** were the most powerful ways to manipulate the *spiritus*. Because sound moves the air, and the *spiritus* is "airy," specific tones and harmonies could draw down angelic (or demonic) influences.

* **How this applies to the Game:** Bell and Harrow didn't invent a computer. They built an acoustic and electrical resonator that acts as a lure for the *spiritus*. The 1985 Hz frequency is literally a Ficinian sympathetic tone that binds the material air of the apartment to the spiritual presence of Ningal.

## 3. Biblical Ontology: Principalities and the Unseen Realm

The Bible routinely speaks of an overlapping, unseen reality that governs the physical one.

* **Ephesians 6:12:** *"For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places."*

* **The Concept of "Heavenly Places" (*Epouranios*):** In Paul's letters, the "heavenly places" are not just where God is; they are the unseen spiritual realm overlapping ours, currently contested by rebellious spiritual beings (like Ningal/Uriel).

* **How this applies to the Game:** Ningal is a "cosmic power" trying to break through. She is using the Aetheric Engine to claim geographic and material authority (starting with Harrow's apartment).

## 4. The Mechanics of Demonic Interaction

According to Christian demonology (and Augustine's *City of God*), demons cannot read human minds or force human will. However, they are master observers.

* **Appealing to the Will:** They can only tempt, manipulate, and appeal to a human's existing desires or intellectual pride.

* **How this applies to the Game:** This is why Ningal appeals to Rowan's strict materialism! Ningal cannot force Rowan to open the door. She must trick Rowan into *choosing* to open it, by hiding her demonic nature behind the language of thermodynamics and computer errors. Ningal weaponizes Rowan's intellectual pride.

### Design Takeaway for the Plot

The machine is not a portal to a sci-fi universe. **The machine is an artificial *Spiritus*.**

Harrow's Faraday cage isn't blocking radio waves; it is a ritualistic boundary that prevents the *spiritus* from expanding beyond the apartment. Rowan's journey from materialism to faith isn't just character growth—it is the literal, ontological requirement to defeat a Principality of the Air.
