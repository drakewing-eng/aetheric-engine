### The Ontological Field: Integrating Victorian Physics and Panpsychism

To deepen the intellectual rigor of your novel, you should upload texts that bridge the gap between **Victorian Electromagnetic Theory** and **Modern Philosophy of Mind**.

In your draft, Ignatius Bell and Edward Pember aren't just "inventors"—they are ontological rebels. They are pushing against the idea that the universe is a "dead machine" (Materialism) and moving toward the idea that the "Field" itself is alive or conscious.

Here is a curated list of works for your NotebookLM, categorized by their relevance to your characters and themes:

### 1. The Historical Foundation (For Bell & Rooke)

**Michael Faraday, ****On the Physical Character of the Lines of Magnetic Force**** (1852):** * Why: This is the "smoking gun" for your 1856 setting. Faraday argues that magnetic lines aren't just mathematical abstractions but physical "tensions" in space. Bell would use this to justify his "Aetheric Engine" as a tool for "stressing" reality.

**William Kingdon Clifford, ****On the Nature of Things-in-Themselves**** (1878):**

Why: Clifford was a 19th-century polymath who coined the term **"mind-stuff."** He argued that even simple matter has a "mental" aspect. Uploading this helps the AI generate dialogue where Rooke or Bell debate whether a machine can "feel" the resonance it produces.

### 2. The Bridge to "Process Philosophy" (For Dr. Harrow)

**Alfred North Whitehead, ****Process and Reality**** (1929):**

Why: Whitehead replaced the idea of "static matter" with **"actual occasions"** (events). He believed everything in the universe "prehends" (feels) its surroundings.

Application: This is perfect for Dr. Harrow’s 2011 notes. Harrow should use Whitehead to explain why the Deveraux Archive "refuses to decay"—because the events are still "prehending" the observers.

**William James, ****A Pluralistic Universe**** (1909):**

Why: James explores "panpsychism" and the idea that our individual consciousnesses are part of a wider "continuum of experience."

Application: Use this to deepen **Elspeth’s** experience. She isn't just seeing ghosts; she is a "narrow opening" to a much wider field of consciousness.

### 3. Contemporary Rigor (For Rowan Ellis & National Book Award Depth)

**Philip Goff, ****Galileo's Error: Foundations for a New Science of Consciousness**** (2019):**

Why: Goff argues that science's biggest mistake was stripping matter of its internal qualities to make it "quantifiable."

Application: Rowan Ellis (2023) should struggle with this. He has the sensors (quantification), but he cannot measure the "felt quality" of the 1 985 Hz frequency.

**Galen Strawson, ****Realistic Monism: Why Physicalism Entails Panpsychism****:**

Why: A heavy-hitting philosophical essay. Strawson argues that if you are a "real" scientist who believes in matter, you must believe matter has a mental aspect, because we know consciousness exists and it has to come from somewhere.

**John McFadden, ****Electromagnetic Field Theory of Consciousness****:**

Why: This is a modern scientific theory (Cemi Theory) that posits our conscious mind is an electromagnetic field.

Application: This directly validates Bell's 1856 experiments. It turns his "mad science" into "cutting-edge foresight."

### 4. Theological/Spiritual Rigor (For Selina)

**The Sandemanian Treatises (John Glas / Robert Sandeman):** * Why: Faraday’s specific sect. They believed in a radically simple, literal Christianity.

Application: This provides Selina with her "Covenant of Order." It’s not just "religion"; it’s a belief that the "Laws of Physics" are the literal "Voice of God."
