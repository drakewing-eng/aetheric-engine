# BEAT 2: THE APARTMENT

*Rowan is inside. The door is sealed. She doesn't know how to leave.*
*This beat is exploration and discovery — no horror yet, just the slow accumulation of a man's obsession.*

---

## 1. THE SEALED DOOR

*[Rowan has just entered. The door closes behind her. A low mechanical suction — then silence. A hum, faint, more felt than heard.]*

**ROWAN**
Professor Harrow?

*No answer.*

**ROWAN**
Charles?

*Silence.*

*Action: Player clicks the front door handle.*

*Rowan turns the handle. Pulls. The door shifts perhaps half an inch — and stops.*

*She tries again. Harder.*

*She checks the deadbolt.*

**ROWAN**
Unlocked.

*She braces a shoulder against the frame and pulls with both hands.*

*The door does not move. Not stuck. Not locked. It feels as though someone on the other side is holding it shut.*

**ROWAN**
*(quietly)*
It's not the lock.

*She lets go.*

**ROWAN**
The pressure.

*The door settles back into its frame with a faint hiss.*

*Only now does she notice the handwritten note taped beside the handle.*

**NOTE:**
> *"To equalize: adjust pressure seal at console. Do not force."*

**ROWAN**
Console.

*She looks back toward the workstation.*

---

He was worried about external interference.

### INTERACTION: WINDOWS *(second click)*

**ROWAN**
Or internal leakage.

## 9. THE COMPUTER DESKTOP

*Three monitors sit in a row:*
- *Monitor 1 (main, largest): dark, sleeping.*
- *Monitor 2 (secondary, same size): dark, sleeping.*
- *Monitor 3 (smaller, older, slightly yellowed): ALWAYS ON. A single green terminal line blinks against black. Separate power source. Running continuously for forty-seven days.*

*Rowan reaches for the main computer mouse. Before she touches it, her eye catches Monitor 3. One green line. Blinking.*

*She looks at it for a moment.*

*Then moves the mouse.*

**ROWAN**
Okay.
Just long enough to send an email.

*She lowers herself into the chair.*
